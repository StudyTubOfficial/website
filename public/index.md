# StudyTub — Free Study Notes for BTECH Students

**URL:** https://studytub.netlify.app/  
**Type:** Educational resource platform

## What is StudyTub?

StudyTub is a free platform that curates high-quality study notes and resources for BTECH (Bachelor of Technology) engineering students. Instead of hunting across the internet, students find all their subject notes in one place.

## Subjects Covered

- Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Mechanics
- Thermodynamics, Materials Science
- Computer Programming, Object-Oriented Programming
- Data Structures and Algorithms, Database Management Systems
- Software Engineering, Operating Systems, Computer Networks
- Digital Signal Processing, Control Systems, Power Systems
- Electronic Circuits, Communication Systems
- Microprocessors and Microcontrollers
- Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics
- Computer Graphics, Web Development, Mobile App Development
- Internet of Things (IoT), Cybersecurity, Data Analytics
- Cloud Computing, Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality

## Key Stats

- Founded: 2019
- Users (2026): ~4,500 students

## Features

- **Free study materials** — Premium-quality notes accessible to all
- **Community contributions** — Students upload and share their own notes
- **Top contributor rewards** — Certificates and goodies for active contributors
- **Regular updates** — Notes reviewed and updated continuously
