# About StudyTub

**URL:** https://studytub.netlify.app/about

## Overview

StudyTub was founded in 2019 with the mission to empower BTECH students with high-quality study materials and make engineering education more accessible. What started as a small collection of shared notes has grown into a community platform serving thousands of students.

## Mission

At StudyTub, we believe every engineering student deserves access to great study materials regardless of their background. We built this platform so students can focus on learning, not on hunting for notes.
