# Team — StudyTub

**URL:** https://studytub.netlify.app/team  
**Page:** Meet the Team

The people behind StudyTub — a dedicated group of engineers, educators, and students working to make quality study materials accessible to every BTECH student.

## What We Do

Our team curates, reviews, and publishes engineering study notes across a wide range of subjects. We also build and maintain the platform that thousands of students rely on every semester.

## Get Involved

Interested in contributing? StudyTub is a community platform — anyone can upload notes, suggest improvements, or join our contributor program to earn recognition and rewards.

- **Contribute notes** — Upload your study materials to help fellow students
- **Report issues** — Help us keep the content accurate and up-to-date
- **Earn rewards** — Top contributors receive certificates and goodies

[Contact us](/contact) to learn more about joining the team.
