# Contact — StudyTub

**URL:** https://studytub.netlify.app/contact  
**Page:** Get in Touch

Have a question, suggestion, or want to contribute to StudyTub? We'd love to hear from you.

## Ways to Reach Us

- **General enquiries** — Use the contact form on this page
- **Contribute notes** — Upload your study materials through your account
- **Report an issue** — Let us know if you find errors in any notes
- **Partnerships** — Reach out to discuss collaboration opportunities

## About Our Response Time

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 2–3 business days. For urgent matters, please include "URGENT" in your subject line.
