## Overview

Free third semester notes organised by branch — computer science, electronics, electrical and instrumentation. Handwritten unit-wise notes plus previous year question papers.

Third semester is where the common first year ends and branch subjects begin. Notes are grouped by branch so you only see what your own syllabus covers.

## Common subjects across branches

Mathematics 3 and Economics are usually taught to every branch in third semester, so those folders are worth checking even if your branch folder is thin. The question paper folder covers all branches in one place.

## What you get in each subject

- Handwritten class notes — unit-wise, shared by students who took the subject
- Previous year question papers — mid-term and end-term
- Lab manuals and records — procedure, readings and sample results
- Reference PDFs and presentations — for quick revision
