# BTECH Notes by Semester

**URL:** https://studytub.netlify.app/notes/
**Type:** Notes index

## Overview

Free engineering notes for every semester — first year through eighth. Handwritten class
notes, previous year question papers and lab manuals for computer science, electronics,
electrical and instrumentation branches. No signup, no payment.

## Semesters

| Semester | Covers |
|---|---|
| [First Year](https://studytub.netlify.app/notes/first-year-engineering-notes.html) | Maths 1 and 2, Physics, Chemistry, C programming, Data Structures, BEE, Engineering Graphics |
| [3rd Semester](https://studytub.netlify.app/notes/3rd-semester-btech-notes.html) | CSE, ECE, EEE, EIE — branch subjects begin |

| [7th Semester](https://studytub.netlify.app/notes/7th-semester-btech-notes.html) | Machine Learning, IOT, Compiler Design, Cryptography |
| [8th Semester](https://studytub.netlify.app/notes/8th-semester-btech-notes.html) | Final semester subjects and project management |

## What you get

- Handwritten class notes — unit-wise, shared by students who took the subject
- Previous year question papers — mid-term and end-term, for every semester
- Lab manuals and records — procedure, readings and sample results
- Reference PDFs and presentations — for quick revision before an exam

## Is it free?

Yes. Every note is free to read and download, with no account required.
