Electives and placements

Machine Learning, IOT and Cryptography are the folders students open most in seventh semester, partly because they overlap with what companies ask about in interviews.

What you get in each subject

  • Handwritten class notes — unit-wise, shared by students who took the subject
  • Previous year question papers — mid-term and end-term
  • Lab manuals and records — procedure, readings and sample results
  • Reference PDFs and presentations — for quick revision

Is this free?

Yes. Every note on StudyTub is free to read and download. There is no premium tier and no account required. StudyTub is built from notes students share — if you would like to add yours, get in touch through the contact page.
