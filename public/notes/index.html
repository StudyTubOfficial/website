What you get

  • Handwritten class notes — unit-wise, shared by students who took the subject
  • Previous year question papers — mid-term and end-term, for every semester
  • Lab manuals and records — procedure, readings and sample results
  • Reference PDFs and presentations — for quick revision before an exam

Which university does this cover?

The first year syllabus is broadly common across Indian engineering universities, so those notes apply almost everywhere. From third semester onward the notes are organised by branch — CSE, ECE, EEE and EIE — and follow a standard BTECH curriculum, with minor differences in unit ordering between universities.

Is it free?

Yes. Every note is free to read and download, with no account required. StudyTub is built from notes students share. If you want to contribute yours, use the contact page.
