What is covered in first year engineering?

Almost every Indian engineering university teaches a common first year across branches. Whether you study computer science, mechanical, civil, electrical or electronics, semester 1 and semester 2 cover the same core subjects. These notes follow that common syllabus, so they work for AKTU, SPPU, VTU, RTU, JNTU and most other universities.

How to use these notes

Before an exam

Start with the handwritten notes for the units your syllabus lists, then work through the solved question papers. Most university exams repeat question patterns year to year, so the solved papers are usually the highest-value hour you can spend.

During the semester

Use the notes alongside your own class notes rather than instead of them. They are most useful for the units where your own notes are thin or the lecture went quickly.

For lab work

The lab manuals include the procedure and sample readings, which helps when you need to write up a record for an experiment you have already performed.
