How to study data structures

Implement each structure once by hand before relying on notes. Most exam questions ask you to trace an algorithm step by step rather than recall a definition, so the dry runs in these notes tend to be more useful than the theory sections.

For interviews, focus on linked lists, trees and sorting — those account for the majority of questions in campus placement rounds.
