## Overview

Free fourth semester notes by branch — computer science, electronics, electrical and instrumentation — with mid-term and end-term papers.

Fourth semester subjects build directly on third semester, and most universities schedule the heavier core papers here. Notes are grouped by branch.

## Using the question papers

The mid-end folder holds both mid-term and end-term papers together. Working through two or three past papers before an exam is usually more efficient than re-reading notes, because it shows which units the paper actually weights.

## What you get in each subject

- Handwritten class notes — unit-wise, shared by students who took the subject
- Previous year question papers — mid-term and end-term
- Lab manuals and records — procedure, readings and sample results
- Reference PDFs and presentations — for quick revision
