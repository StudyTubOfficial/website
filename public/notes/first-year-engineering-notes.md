## Overview

Free, complete notes for BTECH first year — the common semester 1 and 2 syllabus taught across every engineering branch. Handwritten class notes, lab files, and mid-term and end-term question papers.

Almost every Indian engineering university teaches a common first year. Whether you are in computer science, electronics, electrical or instrumentation, semester 1 and 2 cover the same core subjects, so these notes apply regardless of the branch you were allotted.

## Which subjects should you prioritise?

Mathematics and Physics carry the heaviest weight in most first year gradings and are the usual reason students end up with a backlog. If you are short on time before an exam, start with the previous year question papers in the mid term-endterm folder — university papers repeat question patterns closely year to year.

## What you get in each subject

- Handwritten class notes — unit-wise, shared by students who took the subject
- Previous year question papers — mid-term and end-term
- Lab manuals and records — procedure, readings and sample results
- Reference PDFs and presentations — for quick revision
