# First Year Engineering Notes — Free BTECH Semester 1 & 2

**URL:** https://studytub.netlify.app/notes/first-year-engineering-notes.html
**Type:** Study notes index

## Overview

Free, complete notes for BTECH semester 1 and 2 — Engineering Mathematics, Physics,
Chemistry, Mechanics and Programming. Handwritten class notes, solved question papers,
lab manuals and PDFs. No signup, no payment.

Almost every Indian engineering university teaches a common first year across branches, so
these notes apply to AKTU, SPPU, VTU, RTU, JNTU and most others.

## Semester 1 subjects

| Subject | What the notes cover |
|---|---|
| Engineering Mathematics I | Differential calculus, matrices, sequences and series, partial differentiation, multiple integrals |
| Engineering Physics | Optics and interference, lasers, fibre optics, quantum mechanics, crystal structure |
| Engineering Chemistry | Water treatment, polymers, fuels and combustion, corrosion, phase rule |
| Engineering Mechanics | Statics, force systems, friction, centroid and moment of inertia, kinematics |
| Computer Programming | C fundamentals, control flow, arrays, pointers, functions, file handling |

## Semester 2 subjects

| Subject | What the notes cover |
|---|---|
| Engineering Mathematics II | Differential equations, Laplace transforms, vector calculus, complex variables |
| Basic Electrical Engineering | DC and AC circuits, network theorems, transformers, electrical machines |
| Engineering Graphics | Orthographic projection, isometric views, sections, development of surfaces |
| Environmental Studies | Ecosystems, biodiversity, pollution control, environmental legislation |
| Data Structures | Arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, sorting and searching |

## What each subject folder contains

- Handwritten class notes — unit-wise, from students who scored well in that subject
- Solved previous year question papers — with worked answers, not just the paper
- Lab manuals and records — experiment procedure, readings and sample results
- Reference PDFs and presentations — for quick revision before an exam
