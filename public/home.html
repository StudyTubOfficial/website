Which university does this cover?

The first year syllabus is broadly common across Indian engineering universities, so those notes apply almost everywhere. From third semester onward notes are organised by branch and follow a standard BTECH curriculum, with minor differences in unit ordering between universities.

Why StudyTub exists

Good notes usually circulate in a single WhatsApp group and then disappear. StudyTub collects them in one place so the next batch does not have to start from nothing. Everything here was shared by students who took the subject. If you have notes worth passing on, the contact page is the way to add them.
